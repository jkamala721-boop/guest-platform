For guests

Your host shares the link. Hostvero guides the booking steps.

Hostvero is not a property marketplace. You use it when your accommodation host sends you a secure link for a specific booking or stay.

1

Open your guest link

Use only the link sent by your property host.

2

Review and register

Confirm the booking context and provide the guest details required for the stay.

3

Pay when required

Available payment methods are shown securely during checkout. A browser screen alone does not confirm payment.

4

See confirmed status

After verified processing, the booking status and receipt are updated where applicable.

Keep your guest link private

Your link controls access to booking-specific actions. Avoid posting it publicly or forwarding it to people who are not part of the stay.

Need help with the property or booking?

Your accommodation host remains the first contact for property-specific questions. For Hostvero access issues, visit the support page.
